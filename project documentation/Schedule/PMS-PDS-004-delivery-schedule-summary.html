Who does what (condensed RACI)

RoleAccountable for · produces
PO — consultantScope, user coordination, demos, gate scheduling, defect priority, repository handover. The only role that can close a gate is the business users signing it.
QA — consultantTest design and execution, guard negative tests, matrix × route test, PDF snapshots, acceptance evidence. Deliberately independent of the build lane.
DEV (2 people)All implementation. No work-in-progress pushes; history is squashed at handover.
Business usersData provisioning, requirement interpretation, gate acceptance, UAT — 2 half-days per gate.
IT / ownerEnvironment, scheduled task, backups, hosting decisions.
The headline, stated plainly. Inside 01–14 December with two developers and two consultants, this plan delivers Phases 0–3 with Gates 1, 2 and 3 signed — foundation and access, master data and specification, the workflow engine and the NPD spine — plus the supplier-portal shell with proven data isolation, a user walkthrough, a frozen defect list, and the document pack pushed to GitHub. Phases 4, 5 and 6 carry over to the 12 proposed working days above. Compressing all six gates into ten working days would mean dropping the evidence and the sign-off step that produced the approved PRD, FSD and TSD — and the users would see the result only at the end, which is exactly the outcome we were asked to avoid.
