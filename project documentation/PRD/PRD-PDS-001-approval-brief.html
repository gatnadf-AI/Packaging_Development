Decision needed before FSD begins

1. What the system does, and what it replaces

  • Tracks every NPD packaging item through the official NPD flowchart, stage by stage, with a named owner and a recorded decision at each gate.
  • Answers two questions management cannot answer today: which item is stuck, at which stage, waiting on whom — and what was the approved standard and who signed it.
  • Drives the inspection checklist from the stored packaging specification, so T0/T1/T2 results are structured data, not free text.
  • Generates the two documents you already sign — Form Approval Bahan Kemas and Spesifikasi Bahan Pengemas — from data, in the current official layout.

IN SCOPE: the full NPD flowchart (concept + COGS + BOD evaluation, marketing brief & timeline, sourcing, primary branch, secondary/tertiary branch, scale up)  +  supplier portal  +  QC module (T0/T1/T2, application & transport test, colour range)  +  master data, RBAC, notifications, audit trail, exports.

OUT OF SCOPE: the packaging-material substitution process as written in the old Excel (it is not in the flowchart — see OQ-02); EPD as a separate process; ERP/finance integration; legally certified e-signatures; mobile apps.

2. The process the system enforces (primary source: NPD flowchart)

Blue = stage · Red = gate: a decision must be recorded to advance · Green = Scale Up, the exit of that branch. Branches run independently and each closes on its own Scale Up. Nothing before BOD approval of the concept can start.

3. Five decisions that shape the build — please confirm these

Basis of requirementsThe NPD flowchart is authoritative. The old Excel's substitution workflow, its two screen mockups, and the existing prototype are rejected as requirement sources; its role list, access matrix and user stories are kept as secondary input.
Two branches, not one pipelinePrimary and secondary/tertiary packaging progress independently from sourcing, in a different internal order, and each reaches its own Scale Up. A project is complete only when every branch holding an item has closed.
Status is two-axis and derivedEvery record carries a document status (Draft/Submitted/Pending/Closed/Cancelled) and an approval status (Submitted → Checking → Checked → Waiting approval → Approved/Rejected). Approval status is always computed from recorded decisions, never typed by a user.
Approval = attributable decisionSignature blocks on the printed forms are filled from real in-system decisions (name, role, date) and cannot be hand-typed. The author of a record is never an approver of that version; one rejection with a mandatory reason returns it as a new version; approved records are immutable.
Suppliers get a portalSuppliers submit capability, blank samples, dummy photos, quotations (price/MOQ/lead time), artwork and proofprints themselves — with hard isolation: a supplier can never see another supplier's prices, samples or evaluations.
